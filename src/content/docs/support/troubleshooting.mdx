## In-Page Text Expander Not Triggering

If typing a snippet keyword does not auto-expand:

1. **Refresh the Page**: If a web tab was already open when SnippetHub was installed or updated, refresh the tab so the expansion content script is loaded.
2. **Restricted Chrome Pages**: Chrome restricts extensions from running content scripts on internal browser pages (`chrome://*`, `chrome-extension://*`) and the official Chrome Web Store for security reasons. Test expansion on standard websites (e.g., Gmail, GitHub, or any standard form).
3. **Verify Trigger Key & Keyword**:
   - Check the **Trigger** dropdown in the avatar menu to confirm your active trigger prefix (e.g., `;` or `/`).
   - If your keyword is `intro` and your trigger is `;`, type `;intro` followed by `Space`, `Enter`, or `Tab`.
4. **ContentEditable Delimiters**: In certain rich editors (such as Gmail compose or Notion), ensure you follow the keyword with `Space` or `Enter` to finalize the expansion.

## Direct Insertion Doesn't Work on a Specific Website

Certain canvas-rendered documents (such as Google Docs) or nested cross-origin iframes block direct DOM input simulation:

- **Recommended Solution**: Use the **Copy** button (or press `Enter` / `C`), then press `⌘V` (Mac) or `Ctrl+V` (Windows) to paste.
- **Rich Text Preservation**: Because SnippetHub writes multi-MIME payloads (`text/html` and `text/plain`), pasting into Google Docs, Microsoft Word, or Slack preserves all headings, bold formatting, links, and styling.

## Global Shortcut `⌘ShiftP` or `⌘K` Does Not Open

1. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` in your address bar.
2. Scroll to **SnippetHub - Text Snippet Manager**.
3. Check whether another installed Chrome extension is claiming the same keybinding.
4. Assign a new keyboard shortcut or rebind `Open the Command Palette`.

---

## Snippets Not Syncing Across Computers

1. **Verify Account**: Confirm you are signed into the same Google or Email account in the side panel on both computers.
2. **Focus-Driven Refresh**: Simply clicking into a tab or opening the SnippetHub side panel triggers a cache sync with the background service worker.
3. **Token Refresh**: If sync does not resume, sign out and sign back in via the user avatar menu to refresh your Firebase authentication token.

---

## Reverting to Custom Drag-and-Drop Order

If you sorted your snippets alphabetically or by usage and want to return to your manual drag-and-drop order:
1. Click the **Sort Order** button in the list controls header.
2. Select **Default**. Your customized drag-and-drop layout will be restored immediately.
