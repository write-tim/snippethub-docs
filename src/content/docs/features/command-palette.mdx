The **Command Palette** provides an ultra-fast, Spotlight-like search overlay that allows you to search and insert snippets without even opening the side panel.

## Opening the Command Palette

- **Mac**: `Command + Shift + P` or `Command + K` (when in the side panel)
- **Windows / Linux**: `Ctrl + Shift + P` or `Ctrl + K` (when in the side panel)

## How It Works

1. Press the shortcut on any active webpage.
2. The sleek command palette modal appears centered on your screen.
3. Start typing search terms (title, tags, or snippet content).
4. Use the `ArrowUp` and `ArrowDown` keys to highlight your desired snippet.
5. Press `Enter` to insert the snippet directly into the focused field on the page, or press `Escape` to dismiss.

:::tip Customizing the Global Shortcut
You can reconfigure Chrome extension shortcuts at any time by navigating to `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` in your browser.
:::
