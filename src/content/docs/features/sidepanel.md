The **Side Panel** is the primary home for SnippetHub. Built on Chrome's native Side Panel API, it stays open beside your web pages, allowing you to browse and copy snippets without popping open floating windows or switching tabs.

## Key Features

- **Docked Persistence**: Unlike traditional extension popup menus that disappear when you click outside of them, the side panel stays visible while you browse across tabs.
- **Folder Navigation**: Switch between categories (All, Work, Personal, Code, Templates) by clicking the folder tabs or pressing number keys `1` through `9`.
- **Search Bar**: Focus the search bar at any time by pressing `/` or `⌘F` / `Ctrl+F`. Search results update instantly with fuzzy matching powered by Fuse.js.
- **Pinning**: Pin your most frequently used snippets to the top of your list by clicking the pin icon or pressing `P` while a snippet is highlighted.
- **Card Actions**: Every snippet card allows one-click Copy, Direct Insert into the active web field, Edit, Duplicate, Pin, Favorite, or Delete.
