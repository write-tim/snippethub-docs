SnippetHub gives you complete control over your visual workspace, allowing you to tailor the interface to your environment, lighting conditions, and screen size.

## Curated Color Themes

SnippetHub features 4 handcrafted themes with full support for light and dark operating system modes:

- **Default Light**: A crisp, high-contrast light theme with subtle slate borders, optimized for daytime reading.
- **Default Dark**: A midnight dark palette with rich contrast designed to eliminate eye strain during evening workflows.
- **Solarized**: A scientifically calibrated color palette featuring warm dark backgrounds and high-legibility syntax accents.
- **Monokai**: A vibrant developer classic with warm charcoal tones and neon syntax accents, ideal for code-heavy libraries.

### Changing Your Theme

You can switch themes with zero friction directly from the top bar:

<Steps>
1. Click your profile avatar button in the top-right corner of the side panel.
2. Under the **Customization** section, select your theme from the **Theme** dropdown menu (`Light`, `Dark`, `Solarized`, or `Monokai`).
3. The interface transitions instantaneously with zero flicker, and your preference is saved across browser sessions.
</Steps>

## Display Density

Whether you are working on a 13-inch laptop screen or an ultra-wide desktop monitor, SnippetHub offers 3 layout densities:

- **Comfortable (Default)**: Well-balanced padding, spacious action buttons, and clear visual separation between snippet cards.
- **Compact**: Tightly spaced list items with condensed padding, allowing you to see 2–3x more snippets simultaneously without scrolling.
- **Spacious**: Extra breathing room and larger touch-friendly targets, ideal for touchscreen laptops or expansive displays.

To change density, open the avatar menu and choose your preferred mode from the **Layout** dropdown.

## Folder Color & Emoji Accents

Make folder navigation effortless at a glance:
- Assign an emoji icon (e.g., 💻 for Code, 📧 for Email, 🚀 for Projects).
- Assign a color accent swatch (Blue, Indigo, Emerald, Amber, Rose, etc.) or select a custom HEX color.
- Custom colors and icons appear in the folder selector dropdown, on snippet cards, and in folder management menus.
- Configure these directly in the **New Folder** and **Edit Folder** modals.

## Dynamic Tag Colors

SnippetHub automatically colors tags using a deterministic color algorithm:
- Every unique tag (such as `#javascript` or `#sales`) is deterministically mapped to a consistent palette color across all cards and filters.
- You can override colors for specific tags to create your own organizational color system.
- Tags appear as softly tinted chips with subtle borders, maintaining visual hierarchy without cluttering your view.
