Customize SnippetHub to match your browser and desktop aesthetic.

## Available Themes

SnippetHub includes 4 curated color themes:

- **Default Light**: Crisp, clean light aesthetic with high contrast for daytime work.
- **Default Dark**: Midnight dark theme engineered to reduce eye strain.
- **Solarized**: Calibrated palette optimized for readability and code contrast.
- **Monokai**: Vibrant developer-favorite theme with warm dark tones and syntax accent colors.

### Switching Themes
1. Open the user dropdown menu in the top bar.
2. Under the **Theme** section, click your preferred theme.
3. Your selection applies immediately and persists across sessions.

## Display Density

Choose between two spacing modes depending on screen size and preference:
- **Comfortable**: Spacious padding with clear visual separation between cards and controls.
- **Compact**: Tighter spacing allowing more snippets to be visible simultaneously without scrolling.
