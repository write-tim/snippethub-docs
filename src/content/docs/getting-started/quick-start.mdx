Follow this 3-minute tutorial to learn how to create, manage, and use snippets.

## Step 1: Open the Side Panel (or Standalone Window)

Click the **SnippetHub** icon in your browser toolbar or press your configured browser shortcut to open Chrome's native side panel. 

If you prefer a floating window (great for secondary monitors or tiling windows), click the **Pop Out** icon (`⧉`) in the top-right header to open SnippetHub in its own persistent window.

:::tip
You can keep the side panel open while navigating across different tabs, or toggle back and forth between the side panel and popup window at any time without losing your place.
:::

## Step 2: Create a New Snippet

![SnippetHub Snippet Editor](/images/SnippetHub_Add_Snippet.png)

<Steps>
1. Click the **+ New Snippet** button in the top bar (or press `N` on your keyboard when not typing).
2. Enter a descriptive **Title** (for example, `Meeting Follow-Up`).
3. Type your template content into the editor:

```text
Hi {{client:Client Name}},

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today, {{date}}.
As discussed, here is the link to review: {{clipboard}}

Best regards,
Timothy
```

4. Add a tag or assign it to a category (e.g., `Work` or `Email`).
5. Click **Save** (or press `⌘Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter`).
</Steps>

## Step 3: Insert Your Snippet into a Webpage

You have three convenient ways to deploy your new snippet:

<Steps>
1. **Click to Copy / Direct Insert**: Click the **Copy** button on the snippet card or click **Insert** to inject it directly into the active text area or email composer.
2. **Command Palette**: Press `⌘K` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Shift+P` (Windows), type `Meeting`, and press `Enter`.
3. **Omnibox**: Click Chrome's address bar, type `snip`, hit `Space` or `Tab`, type `Meeting`, and press `Enter` to copy it to your clipboard.
</Steps>

If your snippet contains [interactive variables](/features/dynamic-placeholders/) like `{{client:Client Name}}`a lightweight prompt will ask for the client's name before inserting the text!
